About Us

"Welcome to Sunrise Computer Centre, an educational centre situated in the heart of Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh. Immerse yourself in the world of technology as we guide you through the intricacies and potential of computers. Our comprehensive programs, crafted by seasoned educators, empower you with the skills and knowledge to navigate the digital landscape."

Our Mission:

To bridge the gap between traditional education and the rapidly evolving tech industry by providing affordable, high-quality computer education to everyone.

Our Story:

Founded in 2025, Sunrise Computer Centre started with a simple goal:
to make computer literacy accessible. Today, we’ve proudly trained over 100+ students, helping them unlock new career opportunities and navigate the digital age with confidence.
